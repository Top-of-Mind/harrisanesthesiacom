Office-Based Anesthesia for Austin Dental Practices

Austin practices regularly see patients who need general anesthesia — children who can’t complete treatment awake, anxious adults, and long surgical cases. Without an anesthesia provider on staff, those cases get referred out or wait on a hospital schedule.

Dr. Harris comes to your office in Austin and the surrounding communities, brings his own anesthesia, monitoring, and emergency equipment, and manages the case from the first dose until your patient meets discharge criteria.

Services for Austin Practices

  • Pediatric Dentistry — Children who need general anesthesia to complete treatment safely, in your own office instead of a hospital operating room.
  • Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery — Extractions, third molars, grafting, and the other surgical cases your practice lines up for general anesthesia.
  • Periodontics — Periodontal and implant surgery performed with a secured airway, start to finish.
  • Full-Arch & Advanced Implants — All-on-X, zygomatic and pterygoid implants, full-arch placement with sinus lifts, full-mouth extractions with immediate implants, alveoloplasty, and bone block grafting.
  • General & Cosmetic Dentistry — Extensive restorative and cosmetic treatment, and anxious patients who are best treated in a single visit under anesthesia.
