About the Practice

Dr. Brett Harris, DMD

Dr. Brett Harris founded Harris Anesthesia Group to bring a single, consistent standard of anesthesia care to the growing world of full-arch and complex implant dentistry. Rather than dividing attention between surgery and sedation, Dr. Harris serves exclusively as the anesthesia provider — working alongside oral surgeons, periodontists, and prosthodontists to keep patients safe, still, and comfortable through even the longest and most technically demanding cases.

This model allows the operating surgeon to remain fully focused on the surgical field while an independent, dedicated anesthesia provider manages airway, sedation depth, hemodynamics, and recovery — the same division of labor patients would expect in a hospital operating room, brought directly into the surgical office.

  • Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD)
  • Focused exclusively on office-based dental anesthesia
  • Experienced with the full spectrum of full-arch and implant reconstruction
  • Continuous hemodynamic and respiratory monitoring on every case

Procedures We Support

Anesthesia for the Most Complex Cases in Implant Dentistry

Dr. Harris provides in-office sedation and general anesthesia specifically tailored to long-duration, high-acuity surgical cases, including:

01

All-on-X

Full-arch implant-supported restoration requiring extended, motion-free sedation from first incision to final torque.

02

Zygomatic Implant Surgery

Anesthesia support for zygomatic implant placement in the severely atrophic maxilla, where precision and airway control are critical.

03

Pterygoid Implant Surgery

Sedation tailored to posterior maxillary implant placement engaging the pterygoid plates.

04

Full-Arch Placement with Sinus Lifts

Combined implant and sinus augmentation procedures managed with attention to airway positioning and case length.

05

Full-Mouth Extractions with Immediate Implants

Anesthesia for same-day extraction and immediate implant placement, minimizing patient trauma across a single visit.

06

Alveoloplasty with Full-Arch Placement

Bone recontouring combined with full-arch implant surgery, supported by stable, extended sedation.

07

Bone Block Grafting with Simultaneous Implants

Anesthesia care for staged hard-tissue grafting performed concurrently with implant placement.

Safety & Monitoring

A Dedicated Anesthesia Provider for Every Case

Long, complex implant surgeries place real demands on airway management, cardiovascular stability, and sedation depth. Harris Anesthesia Group exists to take that responsibility off the surgical team, providing continuous, dedicated monitoring from the first dose to full recovery.

Continuous Monitoring

Blood pressure, pulse oximetry, capnography, and cardiac monitoring maintained throughout every procedure.

Airway-First Approach

Sedation and positioning planned around airway access for the full duration of extended full-arch cases.

Emergency Preparedness

Full emergency drug kit, advanced airway equipment, and resuscitation protocols on-site for every appointment.

Seamless Recovery

Patients are monitored through emergence and recovery before discharge criteria are confirmed.
